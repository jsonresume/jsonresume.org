# JSON Resume theme collection

## Register

brand

## Users

Job applicants publish their structured resume online and export it for recruiters reading on phones, desktop screens and printed pages. Researchers and international applicants also need longer records and localised labels.

## Product Purpose

Provide ten complete, distinct resume families from the research in `docs/RESUME_THEME_RESEARCH.md`, with the same content working through standalone packages, the CLI, the hosted renderer and the gallery.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed and credible. Each family retains the specific reference's hierarchy, proportions and typography.

## Anti-references

Generic dashboard cards, skill meters, decorative lifestyle charts, tiny pale body text, and layouts that only work with perfectly sized sample content.

## Design Principles

1. Give evidence about the applicant the most useful space.
2. Distinguish families through composition and typographic hierarchy.
3. Preserve content and reading order across screen and print.
4. Treat sparse, long and international resumes as normal inputs.
5. Support user preferences without silently dropping sections.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Target WCAG AA text contrast, semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, text-based qualifications, responsive layouts at 320 pixels, and selectable PDF text. Localise controlled labels when supported; do not claim universal ATS certification.
