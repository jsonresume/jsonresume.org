---
title: Contact details on a resume
description: What a recruiter needs to reach you, what to leave off, and how to keep your phone number and address off a public page.
---

A recruiter needs one reliable way to reach you, and a place to see more of your work. Everything else is optional.

## Include

- **Name**, as you'd like to be addressed.
- **Email**: a plain, professional address.
- **Phone**, if you're happy to be called.
- **City and country**: enough to show whether you're local or open to relocating.
- **A link or two:** LinkedIn, GitHub, a portfolio, a personal site.

## Leave out

- Your full street address.
- Date of birth, marital status, nationality and photos, unless they're customary for the country you're applying in.
- Several phone numbers and emails.

## A public resume, privately

A resume page on jsonresume.org is public. In [Settings](/dashboard/settings#privacy) you can keep your email, phone or street address off the page and its downloads, while your resume keeps them for applications you send yourself.
