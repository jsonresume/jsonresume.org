Under your name sits a headline, `basics.label` in JSON Resume: "Senior Data Engineer", "Registered Nurse, Intensive Care". It's the second thing anyone reads and one of the first things a search matches.

## What makes a good one

- **A title people search for.** "Software Engineer", not "Code Wizard".
- **Your level, if it helps:** Junior, Senior, Lead, Principal.
- **A focus, if it sets you apart:** "Backend Engineer, Payments".
- **Honest about where you are:** if you're changing careers, the title you're moving towards can work ("Data Analyst") as long as the resume backs it up.

## Examples

- Product Designer, B2B SaaS
- Senior Mechanical Engineer, Medical Devices
- Bookkeeper and Payroll Specialist
- Graduate Civil Engineer

## Match it to the job

If you're applying for "Platform Engineer" and you've been doing the job as "DevOps Engineer", your headline can say "Platform / DevOps Engineer". Keep a [version](/dashboard/resume#variants) per direction rather than changing it back and forth.
