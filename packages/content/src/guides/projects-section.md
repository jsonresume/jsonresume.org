title: Showing projects on a resume
description: When a projects section helps, what to write for each project, and how to link to the work itself.
---

Projects show what you can do when nobody told you to: side projects, open source, coursework, or work projects big enough to describe on their own.

## When to include them

- You're early in your career or changing fields.
- A project proves a skill the job needs that your jobs don't show.
- The work is public and impressive.

## What to write for each

- **Name and a link**, if people can see it.
- **One line on what it is**, for someone who's never heard of it.
- **Your part** if it was a team.
- **A result:** users, stars, a launch, a grade, something that changed.

> **Budget Buddy:** a React app for shared household budgets. Built alone over a summer; 1,200 monthly users after a Product Hunt launch. budgetbuddy.example

## Keep it short

Two to four projects, the most relevant first.

In JSON Resume, projects go in `projects`, each with a `name`, `description`, `highlights` and `url`, and every theme shows them.
