---
title: How to write the education section
description: What to include for each qualification, where the section goes, and when to leave dates, grades or high school out.
---

Education is usually short: what you studied, where, and when. Its place on the page depends on how recent and relevant it is.

## For each qualification

- **Qualification and subject:** "BSc, Computer Science" (in JSON Resume, `studyType` and `area`).
- **Institution:** "University of Melbourne".
- **Dates:** the year you finished is enough.
- **Optional:** a strong grade, honours, a thesis or award that matters for the job.

## Where it goes

- **Students and recent graduates:** near the top, before experience.
- **Everyone else:** after experience.

## What to leave out

- **High school**, once you have a degree or a few years of work.
- **Grades** that don't help you.
- **Unfinished study:** list it if it's relevant, with "coursework completed" or the years you studied, never as finished.

## Courses and certificates

Short courses and certifications go under Certificates (`certificates` in JSON Resume), with who issued them and when.
