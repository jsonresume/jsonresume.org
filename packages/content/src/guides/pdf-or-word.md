---
title: PDF or Word?
description: Which file to send: PDF keeps your layout, Word is what some application systems ask for. How to get both from one resume.
---

Most of the time, send a PDF. It looks the same on every screen and printer, and applicant tracking systems read text PDFs well.

## When to send Word

- The job post or application form asks for `.doc` or `.docx`.
- A recruiter wants to edit your resume, for example to add their agency's header.

## When to send a link

A link to your resume page works in an email, a profile or a message, and it's always the latest version.

## One resume, every format

On jsonresume.org your resume is stored once, as data, and served as:

- a page: `jsonresume.org/@you`
- a PDF: `jsonresume.org/@you.pdf` (add `?paper=letter` or `?paper=a4`, and `?fit=1` to fit slightly-long resumes onto one page)
- a Word document: `jsonresume.org/@you.docx`
- JSON, YAML and plain text for other tools

Change your resume once and every format follows.
