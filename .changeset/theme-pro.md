Add the Pro theme: a typeset, single-column serif résumé in Source Serif 4 with a centered masthead, a shared left edge, fine section rules, one consistent treatment for roles, organizations and dates, a shared label track for skills, and designed print pages for A4 and Letter. It renders every standard JSON Resume section from a pure, dependency-free `render(resume)`. Optional `meta.pro` settings control section order, the portrait and the full street address.
