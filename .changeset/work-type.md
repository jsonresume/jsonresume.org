Add optional `work[].type`: how a role was held, as free text. Recommended values are `full-time`, `part-time`, `contract`, `freelance`, `internship` and `temporary`, and they combine ("part-time contract"). It describes past roles, not what someone is looking for. Existing resumes stay valid.
